ProductSnap

What if product judgment could be trained like a muscle, one card at a time?

Who it helps
Product managers who want a daily rep, not another article: a concept, a real story, a quick practice set.
What's live
Live on the App Store and Google Play. Free, no paywall. Built solo in about twelve weeks.

Web app · movie night

FlickMango

What if “where can I watch this?” had one honest answer for Indian films?

Who it helps
Fans who pick their language, region and the services they already pay for, then go straight to watching. No login wall.

Live in Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam and Kannada. Accuracy first, archive second.

Web app · seventh grade math

Run It Back

What if math homework felt less like a test and more like play?

The spark
My son came home one day tense about math tests, stuck on homework that wasn't fun. So I built a friendlier way in: pictures, plain words, hints only when he asks.
Who it helps
Any seventh grader who’d rather try, miss and run it back than sit through a timer.
What's live
Live with every grade 7 topic. No account needed.

The story → runitback.me ↗
