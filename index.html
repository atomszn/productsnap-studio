Mobile app · iOS + Android

ProductSnap

A free learning app that builds product judgment, one card at a time.

Problem
PMs don't need more content. They need better judgment.
Shipped
Flashcards by category, real product stories told as Problem → Move → Result → Takeaway, and a Gym for short practice sets.
Status
Live on the App Store and Google Play. Free, no paywall. Built solo in about twelve weeks.

Web app · streaming discovery

FlickMango

Find Indian films on the streaming services you already pay for.

Problem
Indian films are scattered across services, and where to watch changes with your country.
Shipped
Search by movie or actor, filtered by language, region and your services. Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam and Kannada. No login wall.
Status
Live at flickmango.com. Accuracy first: a verified set of recent hits before a full archive.

Web app · K-12 learning

Run It Back

Seventh grade math, one small step at a time.

Problem
Big math apps give away answers or cover everything thinly. A seventh grader needs room to try, miss and try again.
Shipped
Every grade 7 topic with lessons and practice, opt-in hints and steps, and short three-question “run it back” sessions.
Status
Live at runitback.me. No account: progress saves in the browser, so a kid starts in seconds.

Explainer library · on this site

Pulse

Small picture books for big shifts in tech, AI, money and the economy.

Problem

Shipped
A searchable bookshelf of hand-drawn books: a 60-second summary, page turns, bookmarks, light and dark, phone first.
Status
Live with 30 books on 4 shelves. New titles land after review.
