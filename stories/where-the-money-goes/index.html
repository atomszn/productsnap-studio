$10.6 billion still in the red

Uber is still $10.6B (B = billion) in the hole overall (all its losses minus all its profits since the start) at Dec 31, 2025, down from $32.8B at Dec 31, 2022. In 2025 it made $5.6B of operating profit (profit from running the business).

The twist: about half the profit wasn't from rides

Net income (profit after everything, taxes too) was $10.1B. A $5.0B one-time tax benefit is 49.5% of it (our math, of $10.1B). Uber calls it a “tax valuation release”: credits from past losses booked as an asset once profits look reliable.

So where did the money go?

Of everything customers paid, Uber counted $52.0B as revenue and kept $5.6B as operating profit. Our read: when you see a profit, ask where it came from.
