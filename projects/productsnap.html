ProductSnap

A mobile learning app for product managers.

Status
Live on the App Store and Google Play. Free, no paywall.
Built
Solo, in about twelve weeks of evenings and weekends, with AI as the rest of the team.
Shipped
Build 9 went to both stores.

01 The problem

  • Product managers don’t need more content. They need better judgment, and judgment comes from reps, not reading.
  • [Kumar: add the moment or user that made this problem real for you]

02 What I built

  • Six small modules on one loop: discover a concept, understand it quickly, connect it to a real example, practice the thinking, and come back often enough to build instinct.
  • A deck of 108 flashcards across 9 categories, real product stories told as Problem → Move → Result → Takeaway, and a Gym with five reps per set.

03 Key decisions

  • One idea per module, not a kitchen sink.
  • Stories read top to bottom like a memo, not a card.
  • The Gym shows progress dots so the end always feels close, because quiz fatigue was the thing to design around.
  • Every concept sits in one color-coded Browse screen for muscle memory.
